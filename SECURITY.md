# Security

If you find a security issue in lobstr.io, its API, its SDKs, its MCP server or one of these repositories, tell us
privately, never in a public issue:

- on GitHub, with **Report a vulnerability** in the repository's Security tab;
- or by email, at contact@lobstr.io.

Say what you found, how to reproduce it, and what it exposes. We read every report, tell you once we have reproduced
it, and again once it is fixed, and we credit you if you wish.

Please act in good faith: test only with your own account and data, do not read or change anyone else's, and do not
slow down or disrupt the service.

## Supported versions

The latest major version of the SDKs (`lobstrio` 1.x on PyPI and npm) and of the MCP server. The archived repositories
(the v1 SDK, CLI and n8n node) get no more fixes.
